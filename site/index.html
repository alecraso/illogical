Every pane on every machine,
and anyone on the team can answer.

illogical is a terminal multiplexer for you and your team. Daemons own the terminals, on your laptop, your servers and the team's build box, and they outlive the window, the daemon and the reboot. The browser draws them as tabs and splits you drive with the mouse, or all at once in the swarm, where a failed build or an agent's question lifts out to a card that anyone who may answer can answer, from any screen.

curl -fsSL https://illogical.widgets.wtf/install.sh | sh

Linux (x86_64, arm64) and macOS (Apple silicon). Free and open source, MIT or Apache-2.0.

Whoever is free answers it.

The swarm is one live view of every pane on every machine you and your team can see. Panes cluster by project, machine, kind or person, and the busy ones drift to the middle. Whatever needs someone lifts out to a rail of cards, bundled by cause: "4 failed on build-02", "2 agents ask".

When an agent on any of the team's machines wants to run something or has a question, its card reaches everyone who may answer: on the rail, beside the pane, or as a push notification. The first answer wins, and every card says who gave it ("Allowed by Sam, 14:02"). Then send the agent its next instruction from the same card.

  • Share a session with a person or the whole team, as a viewer or an editor. Revoke it and they're out within a second.
  • Team machines belong to the team: members drive them by their role. A pane on your own machine needs your OK first, for a set time.
  • Claude Code in a plain terminal works too, through its hooks: tool approvals, questions and follow-ups, without typing over whoever is driving.
  • Who did what is kept: every approval, answer and follow-up is in the pane's history under the name of the person who sent it.
  • Private panes never leave your screen, not even as a tile.
  • End to end: illogical control lists your machines and relays them, but never sees what's in them.

Mouse first. No prefix key.

Tabs and splits are ordinary things you click, drag and right-click. Drag a tab onto a pane's edge to split it there, drag a divider to resize, right-click for everything else.

Each finished command gets a mark in the gutter, green or red. Click one to select its output, right-click to copy it or run it again.

Durable, honestly.

Processes don't survive a reboot. Everything else does:

  • Every pane's output is on disk as it happens, and its screen is checkpointed. The layout is saved on every change.
  • After a crash or a reboot each pane comes back with its scrollback and does what you told it: a shell where it was, re-run its command, or run claude --continue.
  • On Linux, restarting or upgrading the daemon doesn't touch running programs at all. vim keeps its screen; a build keeps building.

Agents as blocks, beside your terminals.

Run Claude Code, Codex or any ACP agent as a UI instead of a TUI: its messages, every command it runs with the output, and the cost of each turn.

When it wants to run something, or has a question, you get a card: Approve, Always, Deny, or pick an answer. It waits as long as it takes, survives a daemon restart, and the first answer from any screen wins.

On your phone, from anywhere.

The same live layout on every window you open and on your phone: over your tailnet, or from anywhere through illogical control's relay, encrypted end to end. You get in, and so do the teammates you gave a role. Nobody else does.

A failed build, a long command that finishes, an agent that needs you: you get a push notification. Answer from its buttons where your phone shows them, or tap it to open the card. On a phone the swarm's rail is a strip of cards along the bottom.

A CLI for scripts and agents.

illogical works from any shell and inside every pane. Run things, type into panes, wait for a command or a line of output, tail, and search every pane's history.

The same calls are an HTTP API on a Unix socket.

$ illogical run --wait -- cargo build     # a new tab; exits with its code
$ illogical send %3 'git status' -e       # type a line, press Enter
$ illogical wait %3 --match 'listening on'
$ illogical tail %3 -f --text
$ illogical search 'panic|Traceback' --since 1d
$ illogical agent "fix the failing test"
$ illogical wait %5 --needs-input        # it asks…
$ illogical call %5 approve               # …you answer

Install

One line

curl -fsSL https://illogical.widgets.wtf/install.sh | sh

Downloads the release for your machine, checks it, and starts the daemon as a service (systemd on Linux, launchd on macOS). Run it again to upgrade.

Homebrew

brew tap jhgaylor/tap \
  https://git.inevitable.fyi/jhgaylor/homebrew-tap
brew install illogical
illogicald install

macOS and Linux.

From source

git clone https://git.inevitable.fyi/jhgaylor/illogical
cd illogical
just bootstrap && just install

Needs Rust, mise, just and pnpm. Details.

Then open http://127.0.0.1:7681. From your phone and other machines, put it behind Tailscale (tailscale serve --bg --https=443 http://127.0.0.1:7681), or join it to illogical control to reach it from anywhere and share it with your team (illogicald join https://control.illogical.widgets.wtf). The quickstart covers the rest, and advanced setup covers throwaway VMs per tab, dev servers beside their terminals, more machines, and iTerm2 as a tmux client.
