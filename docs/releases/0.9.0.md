# illogical 0.9.0

Onboarding: every command you need is on screen with a Copy button, a
machine joins you or a team by your choice when you approve it, and a
first run says what to do next (#95–#114, tracker #115).

Install or upgrade: `curl -fsSL https://illogical.widgets.wtf/install.sh | sh`

## New

- **Getting started** (#110). On a first visit a panel covers right-click,
  your phone (the machine's real tailnet URL), control and teams, agents,
  Claude Code (`claude mcp add illogical -- illogical mcp`) and `illogical
  tui`. It's in the session menu and the phone's sheet afterwards.
- **Copy everywhere** (#99). Join commands, team ids, invite and share
  links, the install one-liner and recovery codes (*Copy both*, *Download
  .txt*) each have a Copy button; invite links also Share on phones.
- **Personal or team, chosen when you approve** (#100). The approval page
  says what joining grants and has *Join to: Just me / a team you own*
  (`--team` preselects it). The approving device signs the choice, so
  control can't put a machine in a team by itself. *Cancel* turns the
  join down and the daemon stops.
- **Teams** (#101, #112). The panel explains *watches*, *drives* and
  *owner*, invites have a role picker, and Lock and Remove ask first.
  [docs/teams.md](../teams.md) covers teams, personal vs team machines
  and sharing one session.
- **Names** (#102). A passkey account picks a display name when it's made
  (change it in *Devices and machines…*); teammates no longer see "you"
  or `account-xxxxxx`.
- **Invites without an account** (#103). The signed-out page says who
  invited you to which team, and after asking you see "Waiting for … to
  add you".
- **Owners hear about waiting requests** (#104): a push when a new device
  waits for approval or someone asks to join a team.
- **Add a phone or browser…** (#105): the URL as a QR code and a link,
  with the steps. A turned-down browser gets its own screen.
- **Devices and machines** (#106), grouped into machines and browsers,
  with how many recovery codes are left and *Make new codes*.
- **Agent adapters** (#111). *Start an agent…* says when Claude Code's or
  Codex's adapter (or Node) is missing, shows the command, and *Install*
  runs it in a pane.

## Changed

- **Phones can turn on notifications** (#95): *Notify this device* is in
  the phone's sheet, and turning it on sends a test notification. iOS
  says to add it to the Home Screen first (#96).
- **Control with no machines** has a header with Teams, Devices, Plan and
  Sign out (#97), and its empty state is numbered steps: install, join,
  approve (#98).
- **Owner refusals** say who you are, who the owner is and the
  `illogicald install -- --owner …` that changes it (#109).
- **`illogicald join`, `leave` and `install`** say what's next, how long a
  code lasts, and turn control's errors into sentences (#107).
- **install.sh** checks the daemon answers, prints the PATH line for your
  shell, the machine's tailnet URL when Tailscale is up, and how to join
  control (#108). The README's Tailscale step lists what to turn on first
  (#109), and its MCP allowlist is every tool, kept so by a test (#114).
- **The page** has Copy on every install block and next steps as cards
  (#113).

## Upgrading

Control, the web client and daemons changed together. A daemon from
before 0.9.0 still joins a team when the approver keeps the team it asked
for; if the approver picks *Just me*, upgrade the daemon first.
