# illogical 0.21.0

**Huddles, and panes on Windows.** A huddle is a voice call on a session,
for the people working in it, the way team chat does them: in browsers, on
the phone, and in the macOS, Windows and Linux apps. And illogical now runs
on Windows itself: the daemon, panes that survive restarts, the CLI and the
TUI, installed by the Windows app or by `install.ps1`.

Download: [macOS (Apple silicon)](https://github.com/arugula-salad/illogical/releases/latest/download/illogical-desktop-macos-arm64.zip)
· [macOS (Intel)](https://github.com/arugula-salad/illogical/releases/latest/download/illogical-desktop-macos-x86_64.zip)
· [Linux AppImage](https://github.com/arugula-salad/illogical/releases/latest/download/illogical-desktop-linux-x86_64.AppImage)

## New

- **Huddles** (#242, #291). The headphones button by a session's name
  (in the bar, the phone's bar, or a channel's header in the chat view)
  starts a huddle, or *Start a huddle* in the session menu or palette.
  Everyone with the session sees it's on: the button goes green with how
  many are in, and the chat view's channel shows who. Up to 5 people.
  - The huddle bar stays in the corner while you move between tabs,
    sessions and the chat view: who's in, who's talking, who's muted,
    *Mute* (Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Space) and *Leave*.
  - Audio goes straight between devices, encrypted end to end, through a
    TURN relay when there's no direct path. The machine only introduces
    the members.
  - Through control, each device signs its call fingerprints with its
    device key: one of your own devices shows as *verified*, someone
    else's as *signed*, and a mismatch is refused. Over a tailnet or
    locally, members show as *unverified*.
  - Removing someone's access drops them out at once. A machine restart
    ends its huddles, and the page joins again when it's back.
  - The Linux app runs the call itself (WebKitGTK has no WebRTC): WebRTC
    in Rust, Opus, and WebRTC's echo cancellation and noise suppression.
  - On an iPhone the mic stops while the app is in the background; the
    bar says so.
  - Both ends need 0.21.0: an older machine shows no huddle button.
- **illogical on Windows** (M56–M60, #219–#223). Until now the Windows app
  was only a client for your other machines. Now:
  - `illogicald` runs panes on Windows (ConPTY), and they survive the
    daemon restarting or upgrading, and come back after a reboot.
  - `illogical` and `illogical tui` work there too.
  - The Windows app installs and starts the daemon when none answers, and
    upgrades an older one, as on macOS and Linux. Without the app:
    `irm https://illogical.widgets.wtf/install.ps1 | iex`. Either way a
    scheduled task starts it at logon, with no admin rights.
  - PowerShell gets shell integration (commands, exit codes, the working
    directory, the window title), with no profile or policy change.
  - Agents such as Claude Code are recognized in panes.
  - Notifications are toasts; opening one opens its pane.
- **Lend a device to agents** (S33, #268). *Lend this device to agents*
  in the account panel lets agents on your machines ask to use this
  device's location, camera, microphone or clipboard, or to ask you
  something, through two new MCP tools. You allow or deny each request on
  the page, while it's open. It's an early version, tested on an iPhone.
- **Open a folder in illogical from Finder or Nautilus** (M47, #261).
  Right-click a folder: *New illogical Tab Here* (Finder) or *Open in
  illogical* (Nautilus, installed by the .deb and .rpm). The macOS app also
  opens folders and `.command` scripts as an alternate handler.
- **attach, tui and streams through control** (M49, #254). `illogical
  --host NAME attach`/`tui`, `events --follow` and `tail --follow` work on
  machines reached through control, direct or relayed. Team machines and
  machines shared from another account are listed and reached;
  `illogical hosts` says whose they are.
- **Guest ssh to boxes behind NAT** (#253). A joined machine with no
  address guests can reach makes its ssh invites through control's jump
  host. The session still ends on the machine; control relays ciphertext.
  `share --guest --relay` forces it, `--addr` the direct path.
- **`illogical hooks install`** (#238) adds the Claude Code hooks that put
  an agent's questions, permission prompts and follow-ups on cards, after
  asking. It merges into `settings.json` without dropping anything, and
  `illogical hooks status` says what's there.

## Fixed

- **An answered card you closed came back** whenever you left the tab and
  returned (#295). It stays closed now, and goes by itself after a minute.
- **`illogical --ssh BOX web`** asked this machine's daemon (#252). It
  asks the box's, and prints the link with the `ssh -N -L` forward.
- **The MCP `read_output`** of a pane that closed mid-read now reads the
  rest from the closed pane's record.
- **The app's update upgrades an older daemon** it finds, keeping its
  panes running (#261).
- **Linux bundles build under emulation** (Rosetta, qemu-user), so the
  arm64 packages come from the same script (#261).
