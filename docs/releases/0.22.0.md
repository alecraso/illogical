# illogical 0.22.0

**Chat as a page, invites and mentions, and images into a pane.** Chat
is now a place of its own beside your panes and the swarm, laid out like a
team chat: channels down the left, messages with pictures and a little
Markdown, a composer that completes names, search, and Activity for what's
yours. You can invite a person into a session (or have an agent ask you
to), and a mention reaches its person and says so when it doesn't. And a
screenshot gets into a Claude Code session in any pane, from any client,
the phone included.

## New

- **Chat is a page** (#336, #350). *Panes · Swarm · Chat* in the bar
  switch between the three places. Chat covers the window with its own bar
  (the desktop app's titlebar); your panes stay as they were underneath,
  and *Panes* or Back returns to them.
  - The sidebar has *Activity*, any huddles that are on, and each
    machine's sessions as channels with their panes' threads under them.
    Fold a machine away, show only what's unread, or drag it wider.
  - The workspace menu starts a session and marks everything read.
  - The huddle bar sits in the sidebar's corner.
- **Messages like a team chat's** (#337, #353).
  - Pictures, runs of one person under one heading, a line between days,
    and a red *New* line at the first message you hadn't read. Agents'
    messages carry a badge.
  - A little Markdown: `code`, code blocks, bold, italic, links and
    @mentions (yours stand out). Anything else, HTML included, stays text.
  - Hover a message to quote it in your reply, copy a link to it, or go
    to its pane.
  - The composer grows as you write, completes @names (and `@agent` in a
    pane's thread), and keeps an unsent message with its thread.
  - The header says where the thread is and what its pane is doing, and
    who's in it. ⓘ opens details: the pane's screen, live, and the people.
  - The thread drawer on a pane gets the same messages and composer.
- **Getting around chat** (#338, #360).
  - Search every thread on every machine you can read, as you type.
  - *Activity*: messages that mention you, and agents' answers to you.
  - Ctrl/Cmd+K jumps to a channel. Alt+↑/↓ moves between channels,
    Alt+Shift+↑/↓ between unread ones, and Shift+Esc marks everything read.
- **Invite a person into a session** (#232, #233, #234, #307). *Share
  and notify* in the share dialog, or `illogical invite <who>`, shares a
  session with one person and pushes them alone; you're told whether it
  was delivered, is waiting, or can't reach them.
  - Viewer by default, editor if you say so; on your own machine
    `--drive N` lets them drive for N minutes.
  - An agent can ask (MCP `invite_person`). It always becomes a card that
    only the session's owner can send, and only the owner's own agents
    can ask.
- **Mentions that reach their person** (#296, #297, #307).
  - Tapping a mention's notification opens its thread.
  - Teammates can be mentioned while they're offline.
  - Only an @ that reached someone is highlighted. One that didn't (no
    such person, or `@claude` outside a pane's thread) says so to you,
    under your message.
  - Mentioning someone who can't see the thread offers to invite them,
    to this message on or to the whole thread, and no other.
- **Images into a pane** (#248, #249, #301, thanks to Aaron Biller).
  Paste, drop or attach a file in any client (web, phone, desktop app),
  or run `illogical upload`. It lands on the pane's host (a VM pane's
  machine too) and its path is pasted into the pane, so a `claude` there
  shows `[Image #N]`. Big images are scaled down and HEIC becomes JPEG.
  Files are kept privately for a day, at most 20 MB each and 200 MB per
  machine.

## Fixed

- **The chat view had lost its layout** since 0.21.0: a missing brace in
  the stylesheet hid every chat style, and the phone's request cards from
  agents had lost theirs the same way. The web build now refuses a
  stylesheet with a rule left open.

## Compatibility

- Pictures on messages need the machine on 0.22.0; older messages show
  initials.
- Everything else in chat works against a 0.21 machine.
