# illogical 0.15.0

**Every machine, in the desktop app.** Once your machine is in illogical
cloud, the app shows all of your machines (your account's and your
teams'), each reached directly or through the relay, end to end encrypted,
the way the cloud's page does. Any copy of the app on any machine drives
them all (#159).

Download: [macOS](https://github.com/arugula-salad/illogical/releases/latest/download/illogical-desktop-macos-arm64.zip)
· [Linux AppImage](https://github.com/arugula-salad/illogical/releases/latest/download/illogical-desktop-linux-x86_64.AppImage)
· [Linux .deb](https://github.com/arugula-salad/illogical/releases/latest/download/illogical-desktop-linux-x86_64.deb).
Servers and machines without a screen: `curl -fsSL https://illogical.widgets.wtf/install.sh | sh`

## New

- **The desktop app is a cloud client** (#159, #160). On a machine that's
  joined to illogical cloud (Getting started's *Connect to illogical
  cloud*), the app's window is the cloud's client, with every machine in
  its menu. Before you join, it shows the machine it runs on, as before;
  the tray's *This machine* opens that view any time.
  - **You sign in in your own browser,** where your passkey or GitHub
    sign-in works. *Sign in with your browser* opens the cloud's page,
    which asks "Sign in the app?" with a code to check against the app's
    screen. Allow it, and the app is signed in.
  - **Then approve the app as a new device**, on the browser you just
    used (the prompt comes up there by itself) or any other of your
    devices, as any new browser is. Only your devices can let it in, and
    removing it from your devices cuts it off at once.
  - After that the app stays signed in across restarts.
- **Control: sign-in tickets for apps.** `POST /auth/app` gives an app a
  single-use ticket (ten minutes) that a signed-in browser allows; the
  app's window redeems it for a session. The session alone reaches no
  machine: the app still has to be approved as a device.

## Known issues

- Notifications in the app come from the machine it runs on, not yet from
  every machine.
- The Linux app is x86_64 only; arm64 machines use the terminal install.
