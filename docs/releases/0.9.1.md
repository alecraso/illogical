# illogical 0.9.1

*Move to…*: a machine moves between your account and a team (or between
teams) without leaving and joining again (#100).

Install or upgrade: `curl -fsSL https://illogical.widgets.wtf/install.sh | sh`

## New

- **Move to…** on a machine in *Devices and machines…*, when you own the
  teams on both sides. The members of the team it joins reach it by their
  role; those of the team it leaves lose it at once. Your device signs
  the move and the machine checks it: control can't move a machine by
  itself, or replay an old move. An offline machine moves when it next
  connects.

## Upgrading

Upgrade a machine's daemon before moving it: a daemon from before 0.9.1
doesn't take moves, so control would list it in the team while the
machine keeps to your account.
